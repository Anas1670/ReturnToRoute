Answers to common questions about submitting returns, tracking progress, and reviewing routing decisions.

For customers

How do I submit a return request?

Sign in to your customer account, open My Dashboard, and select an eligible purchase to begin a return request. Provide the requested product details and reason for return.

How can I track my return?

Open your customer dashboard to view your submitted returns, current statuses, and available return history.

What do the return statuses mean?

Submitted means your request was received. Pending Inspection means it is awaiting or undergoing inspection. Pending Approval means a staff decision is awaiting approval. Approved means a route has been authorized. Processing means the return is being handled. Completed means the workflow is finished. Cancelled means the return was stopped.

Does submitting a return automatically approve it?

No. Staff review and approval are separate steps in the return workflow.

For staff

What does Generate Recommendation do?

The rule-based engine considers item condition, packaging condition, and functionality to suggest a route such as Restock, Refurbish, Recycle, or Dispose. A recommendation is not an automatic approval.

Can I choose a route manually?

Yes. The Manual Decision option lets authorized staff select a route and provide an explanation without first generating a system recommendation.

Can I change a generated recommendation?

Yes. During approval, authorized staff can accept the recommendation or choose another route and explain the change.

Accounts and access

How do I reach the correct dashboard?

Select My Dashboard from the homepage. Signed-in customers are directed to the customer dashboard and signed-in staff to the staff dashboard. If you are signed out, you will be directed to Sign In.

Can I access my account after signing out?

Yes. Use Sign In on the homepage and enter your account credentials again. Signing out ends your active session.
